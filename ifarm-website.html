About Us

Rooted in Agriculture.
Built on Data.

iFarmTech Ltd. is an innovative Malawian company operating at the intersection of agriculture and technology. We are dedicated to producing agricultural products while simultaneously building a comprehensive tech platform to connect and empower Malawi's entire agriculture economy.

Our distinctive strategy begins with hands-on farming and small-scale agro-processing, allowing us to generate revenue and gather crucial real-world data from the ground up. This data-first approach is foundational to developing tailored software solutions aimed at significantly scaling productivity, enhancing market access, and improving financing opportunities for farmers in Malawi and, eventually, beyond.
