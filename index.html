About Us

Rooted in Agriculture.
Built on Data.

iFarmTech Ltd. is an innovative Malawian company operating at the intersection of agriculture and technology. We are dedicated to producing agricultural products while simultaneously building a comprehensive tech platform to connect and empower Malawi's entire agriculture economy.

Our distinctive strategy begins with hands-on farming and small-scale agro-processing, allowing us to generate revenue and gather crucial real-world data from the ground up — developing tailored software solutions that scale productivity, enhance market access, and improve financing opportunities for farmers in Malawi and beyond.
